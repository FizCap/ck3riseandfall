# CK3 Titles, Succession, Offices, and Realm Changes

Read this guide when changing title laws, succession, diarchies, court positions, realm splits, bordergore, or title/vassal transfers. General scope and token checks are in `ck3-scripting.md`.

## Laws and persistent state

- CK3 1.20 separates `common/law_groups/` from `common/laws/`. Each law is a top-level definition with `law_group_type` and a unique group-local `index`; nesting laws inside their old group definitions leaves law IDs unresolved. Compare copied laws against installed `common/laws/_laws.info` and the current vanilla definitions after upgrades.
- CK3 1.20 appointment types use `allowed_candidate_tier = lower_or_equal` instead of `allow_same_tier_candidates = yes`. Administrative candidate scoring now includes shared `appointment_score_*` values; retain those vanilla calls before appending feature-specific adjustments.
- For an explicit title-level active-state marker, treat that marker as authoritative. Succession, trait assignment, and maintenance may preserve marked state, but must not recreate a cleared marker from stale character traits.
- Apply temporary realm succession laws to the ruler whose realm enters the state. Store the previous law on a persistent primary-title variable, then restore only after the triggering state has ended.
- Script-only laws must set `should_start_with = { always = no }`; `can_have` alone may not prevent default-law selection.
- Temporary inheritance laws that exclude landed candidates should use native `exclude_rulers = yes`; a negative score does not make a landed candidate ineligible.
- For low-stability recovery chance multipliers, do not seed incident cooldown globally at `on_game_start_after_lobby`. Apply recovery on incident completion so initial risk follows actual stability.
- For generated Legitimists camps selected from an implicit claim, add an explicit claim on the prepared target and set vanilla `legitimist_claimed_title` before applying the camp-purpose law. Repair both before war discovery; forcing only the law allows vanilla `can_keep` to replace it.

## Diarchies and script-owned court positions

- A native puppet type's `interaction` is its setup/selection interaction, not a command launcher. The Puppets panel's interaction-category icons describe available actions. Actual character commands use the target's interaction menu: `GetAvailablePuppets` supplies side tabs, `SelectPuppet` selects the acting puppet, and the header uses `GetCurrentPuppetCharacter` to show "Acting as". Hide setup for already-connected targets and explain this target-first flow in the type description.
- CK3 1.20 native puppets use `common/puppets/types/` and `common/puppets/actions/`. Type `can_have` evaluates on the master; `is_valid` also exposes `scope:puppet` and runs daily. `set_puppet` fails for an already-puppeted target or an occupied type slot. Clear only the feature-owned type during replacement/removal, and give custom commands their own action IDs as directed by vanilla `_puppet_types.info`. Vanilla secular acquisition requires By God Alone and an AI recipient. Runtime testing also confirmed that `set_puppet` rejects an ordinary unlanded courtier as a master even with custom type eligibility. Guard native setup with vanilla `can_have_puppets_trigger` (adult advanced ruler, count-tier title, non-mercenary/non-herder); retain scripted regency powers for ineligible diarchs. This courtier finding does not establish eligibility for every form of landless ruler.
- Persistent diarchy states need a dedicated marker and explicit removal effect. Position sync and missing-candidate recovery may repair/defer the office, but never end the diarchy implicitly.
- Keep native puppeteer lifecycle hooks independent of the low-stability incident rule: disabling new incidents must not disable synchronization of an existing Puppet Regency. Diarchy/title hooks repair the affected ruler only; game-load and yearly handlers may scan existing rulers. Retain the shared court-position sync call so its delayed teardown repair also refreshes native control.
- Vanilla `player_change_on_actions.txt` documents `on_player_character_change` with the new player as `root` and the old one as `scope:previous_player_character`; it clears the new player's native puppet status. Refresh custom master/ruler bindings when switching characters rather than assuming a yearly maintenance pass has already run.
- A court-position container tied to the state must remain shown and valid from the persistent marker during transient inactive/type-less succession gaps. Put active-diarch requirements on `valid_character`, rather than tearing down and recreating the position.
- Keep a script-owned position’s `ai_position_score` clearly below vanilla’s hire threshold and the holder’s firing score safely positive; boundary scores can race scripted succession. Before each scripted appointment, call vanilla `can_appoint_char_to_court_position` in the hiring liege’s scope.
- Do not appoint from `on_court_position_received`. Coalesce revoked, invalidated, and vacated callbacks behind one delayed, marker-guarded resync; block fresh creation until engine teardown finishes.
- On an active diarch’s death, save documented `diarchy_successor` before cleanup can end the diarchy. Do not choose during the transient empty-diarch callback. After teardown, retain a valid engine-advanced diarch, otherwise restore the saved native successor; use a custom fallback only if that successor is missing or invalid.

## Title and realm mutations

- Administrative noble-family titles can be county-tier without territorial land. The 2026-10-03 post-coup save showed `c_nf_zhao_8d99_1` used as the loyalist county influence seed, while the fleeing ruler lost both real counties and his dynamic hegemony was destroyed twice. Filter county title loops with `is_noble_family_title = no` and `is_landless_type_title = no` when selecting retreats, counting donor spare counties, collecting personal territorial votes or building the county graph. `tier = tier_county` alone is insufficient; character `is_landed = yes` does not filter their held family title.

- `debug_log` localization has ROOT/SCOPE/PREV contexts; direct saved-name event-tooltip expressions failed in the Palace Coup runtime log on 2026-10-03. Prefer `debug_log_scopes = yes` to inspect typed saved targets and engine IDs, and cap repeated dumps. This differs from event text, which supports direct saved-name expressions.

- Runtime Palace Coup logs on 2026-10-03 showed `got_minister_position_effect` nesting `ResignInvalidTierNonDejure` during crown transfer, followed by an unheld dynamic successor and a failed `set_primary_title_to`. Vanilla `on_title_gain` refills the ministry on an incoming emperor from another house; its minister promotions can retire territorial titles. Protect recorded landed participants from that recruitment while transferring their camps, and verify a generated crown still belongs to its intended ruler after native callbacks before generating its CoA or selecting it as primary. A mock transaction passing does not establish that native nested callbacks preserve the titles.

- Generated `docs/effects.log` requires `government_base` on granted title moves to unlanded holders; use a landed character whose government they should copy. Vanilla Celestial government also sets `admin_allows_holding_multiple_primary_tier_titles = no`, unlike ordinary administrative government. Do not assume the two governments accept the same intermediate crown holdings; queue a replacement crown and removal of the original in one title-change batch. The effect batches moves, but final office and allegiance behavior still requires in-game validation.

- Custom governments must also be listed in `NGovernment.GOVERNMENT_TYPES` under `common/defines/`; CK3 uses this list to preregister their opinion and contribution modifiers. Preserve the installed vanilla list when overriding this array and append custom IDs. A government definition alone leaves missing-modifier errors at startup.
- `set_landless_title = yes` requires the title to already have a preferred capital. Set it first with title-scoped `set_capital_county = <county title>` (as vanilla religious-head creation does); this sets a reference location without transferring county ownership. After resolving the title grant, verify the intended holder and `is_ruler = yes` before calling `change_government`.
- `create_dynamic_title` rejects county and barony tiers at validation ("Tier must be greater than county"). Use duchy or higher for dynamic offices, and ensure the office's intended liege outranks it if it must remain a vassal.
- Snapshot target titles/counties before resolving title or vassal changes inside title/realm iterators. Mutate in a separate `every_in_list` pass; changing live collections can invalidate vanilla on_action scopes.
- For delayed Palace Coup vassal choices, the event root remains the answering vassal even when a scripted effect enters the incumbent's scope. Use the saved incumbent/challenger scopes throughout title handoff, payouts, rivalries and follow-up events. Consolidate claimant bids before opening supporter choices, and redirect consolidated bidders to the retained leader.
- `every_vassal` / `any_vassal` visit only direct vassals; `every_vassal_or_below` / `any_vassal_or_below` traverse the hierarchy (generated effects/triggers documentation). Direct-only allegiance cannot divide a large nested subrealm into provincial fronts. Snapshot county adjacency and each eligible ruler's personal counties before transfers; grow simultaneous county catchments from competing rally points before scoring ordinary AI. Preserve explicit choices and firm personal ties. Reparent only branches whose chosen camp differs from their original liege, retaining same-side hierarchy. Secure the incumbent's retreat county before transferring the capital core to preserve landed government.
- A coherent county catchment does not guarantee coherent allegiances: political locks can still create isolated pockets. Ordinary AI ties should influence votes without seeding distant rally points. For a bounded pocket cleanup, label the final same-side county components in simultaneous passes; protect player/principal holdings and rulers whose personal counties span components before moving an intact AI supporter. Inspect actual ownership before treating every visible island as an AI-allegiance issue.
- For a Palace Coup peer-realm handoff, queue the incumbent successor crown together with removal of the original crown and transfer of the captured core. Select both final primary titles after resolving the batch, and generate dynamic CoAs after their holders exist. Realm Split grants crowns to different rulers, so its separate successor-grant ordering does not establish that a deposed ruler can temporarily hold two crowns.
- After vanilla `depose_effect`, let normal succession distribute surviving titles unless the mechanic explicitly requires another holder. A second manual transfer can override or duplicate inheritance.
- If every county of an administrative sub-vassal is reassigned, transfer the intact ruler to the destination liege instead of unlanding them. For mixed holdings, surrender only offending counties.
- Bordergore eligibility and execution must share the same county triggers. Direct holdings use the holder’s primary de-jure region plus every personally held title in the county’s de-jure chain. Subordinate holdings use the target’s equivalent region. An intact transferred ruler then surrenders counties outside their own region while retaining a core county.
- For a standard realm split, choose successor anchors from the full eligible vassal pool. Assign ordinary vassals individually using political/geographic influence; de-jure regions are soft cohesion and fallback aids, not mandatory anchor quotas.
- When a Story Mode event genuinely fragments a realm, apply bounded collapse-pressure relief after transfers and resync the primary-title copy. Structural weakening should reduce an arc without deleting it.
