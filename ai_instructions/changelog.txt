CK3: Rise and Fall - 1.2

- Fixed AI title cleanup evaluating succession laws in a title scope, and county handouts generating a courtier with conflicting location and employer data.

AI Title Management
- Limited Advanced AI title cleanup to one safe secondary crown per ruler each year, prevented it from destroying an empire merely because its holder serves a hegemon, and distributed title management across the year instead of processing the entire world at once.

Warlord Crisis
- Reworked Warlord crises so their strength and recruitment now scale with the territory controlled by Warlord vassals and their subrealms.
- Made the initial Warlord uprising strongly favor vassals joining the movement, with loyal or cautious personalities providing the main exceptions.
- Made isolated minor Warlords increasingly likely to abandon their movement, while powerful Warlord blocs can attract new followers and endure.
- Made vassals bordering a fellow Warlord more likely to join the movement for local protection and alliance opportunities.
- Removed automatic Warlord claims on liege titles; Realm Split now remains the route for an internal Warlord crisis to fracture a realm.
- Stopped Warlord status from automatically passing to heirs and preserved retired Warlord handling for compatibility mods.
- Updated Realm Split to require a Warlord vassal and a major vassal controlling one fifth of the realm.
- Made Warlord Conquests target only neighbouring de-jure kingdoms, resolve the selected kingdom through vanilla's complete-subrealm transfer path, give administrative-style losers claims on surrendered titles, and override the five-year truce with one year only when the victor started smaller.

Realm Splits
- Fixed voluntary successor-state surrenders so non-titular crowns are retained while obsolete titular successor crowns are retired after the handoff.
- Made hegemony-scale breakaway realms form their own dynamic hegemony, allowing their empire-tier successor states to remain vassals.
- Made Palace Coup retreats from hegemonies use the same safe dynamic-title handoff, so loyal empire-tier realms can rejoin the deposed hegemon.

Palace Coup
- Made Palace Coup vassals choose between the deposed ruler, coup leader, and every landed direct-vassal claimant; claimant supporters now form claimant-led breakaways or allied successor states at the displaced title's rank.
- Made Palace Coup player side selection show the deposed ruler, coup leader, and all direct-vassal claimants in one paginated list; AI now favors allied, well-liked, dynastic, cultural, religious, and regional claimant ties before strength.
- Made Palace Coup claimants declare their own bids before supporters choose sides, so the player and AI supporter lists contain only claimants who actually entered the contest.
- Fixed Palace Coup retreats so the deposed ruler loses only the capital county and throne, retaining every other personally held title and their government type; one-county rulers receive a replacement county before the capital handoff.
- Added a dynamic government fallback for one-county Palace Coup rulers when no replacement county can be found before the capital changes hands.
- Fixed Palace Coup successor treasuries and claimant title handoffs so tooltip evaluation no longer emits errors, empty treasuries are not transferred, and new claimant titles generate their coats of arms after receiving a holder.
Result : Palace coup war scenario should be a lot more dynamic now due to ai being able to evaluate all the contenders for the throne rather than just the old liege and the palace coup leader.


Successor State Wars
- Restricted Successor State Wars to land-neighbouring de jure kingdom regions controlled within the defending realm; the kingdom title itself no longer needs a holder.
- Fixed selected-kingdom war previews treating the defender's entire de facto realm as guaranteed territory; objectives now highlight only the selected kingdom's de jure land.
- Made smaller Successor State War victories transfer the selected defender-held de jure kingdom in one safe batch, preventing split-realm rulers from temporarily becoming landless during peace resolution.
- Made AI successor states use a strength-scaled war chest: overwhelming foes can be attacked immediately, while close contests still require substantial reserves.
- Kept forced AI Successor State Wars on a random neighboring kingdom target while revalidating that selected target through the CB's normal declaration checks.
- Replaced occupation-based Successor State War border settlements with de-jure kingdom war-goal transfers; fixed their objective overlay and ticking score to use the selected kingdom rather than the defender's full realm; preserved a county for any defender who would otherwise become landless; a larger successor now vassalizes the defeated successor when rank permits; and smaller victors receive a one-year truce instead of the normal five years.
- Removed the custom 51% occupation concession path; vanilla's complete-subrealm eligibility now transfers a qualifying vassal branch whenever it remains entirely inside the selected kingdom.
- Made voluntary successor-state surrenders hand an equal-or-higher titular crown to a recipient without a titular title, preserving the successor realm and its vassals instead of destroying that crown.
- Stopped AI title cleanup from destroying crowns under gavelkind or partition succession, and preserved France and Aquitaine while making AI holders create their counterpart for unification.
- Fixed selected-kingdom transfers trying to grant capital baronies directly; county-or-higher titles now carry their baronies safely while complete vassal sub-realms transfer intact.
- Split Successor State Wars into kingdom skirmishes for equal-or-smaller realms and full-realm absorption wars for larger realms; split Warlord Conquests by the same local-realm-size rule.
- Shortened Successor State War ticking-score delay and increased its pace once a war-goal threshold is secured.
- Kept white peaces and attacker surrenders as non-territorial outcomes instead of applying the old occupation-based status-quo concessions.
Result : Successor state wars should be a lot shorter, they are sort of like skirmishes now with dynamic peace deals. AI should be more aggressive with declaring successor state wars now.

Warlord Conquests
- Restricted Warlord Conquests to neighbouring de jure kingdom titles held within the defending realm.
- Reworked Warlord Conquest outcomes around the selected neighbouring kingdom, with intact vanilla vassal transfers, government-specific defeated-ruler handling, size-sensitive truces, and no extra peer-crown destruction.

Coalition Wars
- Made kingdoms and other territory absorbed through victorious Forced Vassalization and Subjugation wars count as recent conquest for Coalition Threat, without penalizing peaceful offers of vassalage.
- Made rising Coalition Threat progressively reduce how often most AI rulers declare ordinary offensive wars and lengthen their war cooldowns; aggressive personalities resist the pressure, while Ambitious rulers and Warlords ignore it.
- Allowed regional powers controlling at least 1% of the world to accumulate warning Threat at reduced strength, while preserving the 5% world-share requirement for an actual Coalition declaration.
- Fixed Medium Coalition verdicts releasing intact peripheral vassals from unselected kingdoms; concessions now remain limited to the largest selected peripheral kingdoms needed to satisfy the verdict.
- Made Coalition Threat and its active crisis window follow an inherited primary crown, preventing succession from erasing the geopolitical consequences of recent conquest.
- Made completed Coalition wars consume the current Threat crisis and protect the target crown from immediate repeat Coalition wars, with longer settlements after harsher verdicts.
- Reduced AI voters' automatic preference for Heavy punishment merely because they are individually weaker than the defeated target, making relationships and personality more decisive.
- Made Independent Coalition declarations recheck the shared 25% peripheral-territory requirement and exposed that requirement in the interaction checklist.
- Refined the Coalition Threat panel into direct Threat Change, Conquest Modifiers, and net yearly result sections, making the political reasons for each annual change easier to read at a glance.
- Made Coalition Threat build only from recent conquest, with foreign de jure territory, realm scale, global power, and neighboring dominance now amplifying each conquest instead of creating passive Threat for peaceful large realms.
- Made Coalition Threat hold at 100 for a five-year Coalition window, then reset to zero if no Coalition forms; a declared Coalition pauses the timeout for the duration of its war.
- Redesigned the Stability-panel Coalition Threat display with a colour-coded meter, warning icon, coalition-status message, and a yearly breakdown of conquest, foreign territory, regional dominance, global power, and peaceful decay.
- Replaced Independent Coalition's one-time expansion checks with a visible Coalition Threat meter that accumulates from rapid conquest, falls during peaceful years, and allows Coalitions at 100 Threat.
- Fixed intact-vassal Coalition releases reading the defeated ruler's whole realm instead of each vassal's own counties, which forced every peripheral vassal into peasant shards; also stopped Heavy punishment from attempting to transfer capital baronies directly.
- Fixed Coalition breakup tooltip errors from an unset temporary global county counter.
- Fixed Coalition breakup scope cleanup interrupting the intact-vassal release path, and corrected Coalition invitation and tooltip-preview scope errors reported by the game log.
- Fixed Coalition peripheral breakups to release intact direct-vassal realms such as Italy under their existing rulers; peasant-led shards now form only for peripheral land that cannot be released as a whole vassal realm.
- Fixed Independent Coalition overextension eligibility being left stale when Low Stability Events were disabled; the county percentage now initializes on load, refreshes yearly, and updates when territory changes hands.
- Made player-started Coalitions wait for every invitation reply, then show the final committed attacker and defender strength before the player chooses whether to declare war or stand down.
- Made the Start Coalition interaction show its complete green-and-red eligibility checklist instead of displaying only failed requirements.
- Added Independent Coalitions (Beta): independent players and AI can use a character interaction to gather a Coalition against a large, overextended realm, with a ten-year failed-formation cooldown on the target's primary title.
- Made the Independent Coalitions (Beta) interaction unavailable when Lite Mode is enabled.
- Added Coalition interaction failure explanations and prevented larger realms from starting Coalitions against smaller realms.
- Allowed Coalitions to target eligible rulers without requiring a primary heir.
- Split Coalition low-stability adapters from the shared/main Coalition scripts so the two formation routes can be maintained independently.
- Restored Coalition victory ballots so every attacker votes once in randomized order and the Coalition leader resolves tied verdicts; Light now demands a three-year indemnity, Medium adds largest-first peripheral concessions of at least half the original outer realm, and Heavy adds abdication plus a full peripheral breakup while preserving the original crown and core.
- Reworked Coalition Wars into instant containment crises for unstable, overextended great powers; eligibility now measures the percentage of every realm's counties outside its primary title's de jure core.
- Fixed Coalition core territory to use only the primary title's exact de jure county list, so separately de jure empires such as Francia remain peripheral to a Holy Roman Emperor.
- Fixed Coalition fallback rerolls losing their ruler scope while calculating peripheral territory, which could prevent a valid Coalition from gathering its cause.
- Fixed Coalition fallback rerolls attempting a county calculation from an empty script scope, corrected de-jure-claim invitee checks, and supplied valid gender data for peripheral shard rulers.
- Fixed selected Coalition incidents rechecking temporary cause variables during assembly; once a valid Coalition fires, it now proceeds unless its target genuinely becomes invalid.
- Fixed Coalition's yearly county counter to retain the pulse ruler across nested de-jure iterators, preventing empty-scope variable errors.
- Reworked Coalition's county counters to use short-lived global scratch values because this CK3 build drops character scopes inside nested county iterators.
- Restored Coalition invitations to every independent ruler in diplomatic range; borders, claims, culture/faith, relations, and military outlook now weight acceptance rather than blocking invitations. Coalition attackers must still exceed the defender's alliance strength, and Coalitions retain their fifteen-year cooldown.
- Restored Coalition white peace and reduced failed internal rebel punishment to imprisonment plus one tyranny-free revocation.
- Fixed Coalition victory ballots using the wrong scope, which could spam errors and apply Heavy punishment before any vote.
- Fixed Coalition vote tooltip errors and ensured Heavy votes release only eligible foreign direct vassals.
- Made the first Coalition victory ballot open immediately after the war ends.
- Made Medium and Heavy Coalition verdicts also distribute the full Coalition indemnity before their title punishments.
- Fixed Coalition indemnities using an invalid negative gold effect; payouts now transfer directly from the defeated ruler, and the defeated player sees the verdict immediately on surrender.

War Enhancements
- Fixed family-revenge war events after a ruler's death when no eligible heir or family member can lead the rebellion, preventing unset-scope errors.

Authority Collapse
- Made Total Collapse require overlapping unresolved nationalist and vassal crises under catastrophic stability pressure.

Stability System
- Set the Low Stability Event cooldown to ten years and kept it on the primary title, so it persists through succession.
- Set Nationalist Revolt, Vassal Authority Crisis, and Total Collapse effects to last ten years; their escalation markers now preserve the path to Total Collapse across the three ten-year cooldown windows.


Developers Notes
This should be a massive update when it comes to the ai stability of this mod, I noticed countries were collapsing to fast so I increased the low stability cooldown to 15 years to let the other low stability events ferment a bit and let the drama play out. 
I noticed the ai was still pretty bad with successor wars and potentially uniting countries, so ai has more improved parameters for when to declare war and successor cb was divided into two, minor skirmish and full on conquest.
I'm hoping these changes will improve what this mod is trying to accomplish.
