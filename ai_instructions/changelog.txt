CK3: Rise and Fall - 1.2.4

Coalition
- Changed AI Coalition caution to react to net yearly Threat growth, while keeping accumulated Threat for Coalition eligibility and preserving severe caution during the active Coalition window.
- Stopped Palace Coup and Realm Split title handoffs from being recorded as conquests and falsely inflating Coalition Threat.

Realm Stability
- Added tiered Cultural Cohesion: foreign cultures with 50-59% acceptance receive partial integration, 60-69% receive strong integration, and 70%+ are fully cohesive.

Administrative Appointments
- Renamed the Warlord child appointment bonus to "Child of Warlord" and added a 690-point bonus for Warlord relatives.

Warlord System
- Removed liege dread as a factor in vassal loyalty and rebellion decisions.
