CK3: Rise and Fall - 1.2.5

Warlord System
- Made military strength relative to the liege progressively reduce loyalty and increase rebellion as a vassal becomes stronger.
- Restricted Warlord succession bonuses to children and close family of Rebel Warlords; Loyal Warlords now use regular appointment succession scoring.
- Limited Celestial Governor succession candidates to the holder's close family and removed the Japanese Regent, Meritocratic Regent, and Celestial Minister appointment definitions.
- Added a temporary close-family succession law for Japanese, Meritocratic, and Celestial realms during Rebel Warlord crises, with automatic restoration of the prior vanilla law afterward.
- Removed redundant Japanese, Meritocratic, and Celestial governor appointment overrides so those realms use the vanilla definitions outside Rebel Warlord crises.
- Prevented the hidden Warlord succession law from being selected as the default succession law for supported administrative realms.
- Restored the complete vanilla succession-law group while keeping the temporary Warlord law as a script-only addition, preventing normal administrative succession laws from becoming invalid.
- Fixed Warlord succession restoration from yearly on-actions by preserving the ruler scope while changing laws through the primary title.
- Added direct age scoring to the temporary Warlord appointment, giving candidates one point per year of age.
- Prevented the temporary Warlord succession law from applying to independent rulers and forced independent realms back to their appropriate vanilla law.
- Fixed the player Warlord Path decision options so Loyal and Rebel alignment changes can be selected during an active era.
- Fixed the Warlord Path confirmation effect so the selected alignment is applied from the character's current Warlord trait.
- Replaced the non-committing Warlord Path option widget with a normal executable toggle decision.
- Fixed Warlord Path requirements using tooltip-safe variable existence checks so the decision can be confirmed.
- Allowed the player Warlord Path toggle to work from the Warlord trait even when no Warlord Era marker was initialized.
