CK3: Rise and Fall - 1.2.3

Successor State Wars
- Preserved the winner's proper primary title after a transfer, then retired every ordinary secondary titular crown of equal or lower rank along with obsolete saved successor crowns.

Advanced AI Title Management
- Retired secondary titular titles for basic and advanced partition-succession AI rulers while preserving ordinary crowns for inheritance.

Realm Stability
- Replaced the 500 gold reward for peasant leaders in Total Collapse and Nationalist Revolt releases with a two-year full discount on title creation costs.

Warlords
- Invalidated ongoing Warlord wars when the attacker no longer had a Warlord trait.
- Prevented inherited or stale Warlord traits from restarting a Warlord Era after its title-level era state had ended.
- Preserved Rebel or Loyal Warlord alignment when an active-era vassal title passes to an heir.
- Added a decisive succession-score bonus for direct children of Warlord administrative, celestial, meritocratic, and Japanese administrative governors.
- Restricted Forge Warlord Titles to uncreated titles whose de-jure counties are majority-controlled by the Warlord or their sub-vassals.
- Prevented Forge Warlord Titles from creating unclaimed titles with no county controlled by the Warlord or their sub-vassals.
- Granted Rebel and Loyal Warlords a full discount on title creation costs.
- Automatically retired inherited legacy Warlords after five years while preserving their temporary regional behavior and keeping them outside the receiving realm's active crisis.
- Kept Warlord banners inherited from a separate kingdom as legacy factions unless the receiving realm was already in an active Warlord Era, preventing a local crisis from spreading across an empire.
