CK3: Rise and Fall - 1.1.1

Palace Coup
- Restricted Palace Coup retreat breakaways to vassals with a claim on the deposed ruler's primary title; other vassals now join either the coup or the fleeing ruler.
- Made claimant vassals weigh their own Palace Coup rebellion against loyalty to the deposed ruler and support for the coup, instead of always breaking away.
- Prevented the Palace Coup retreat outcome from invoking the wider realm-collapse system and creating unclaimed extra successor states.
- Made Palace Coup challengers seize half of a fleeing ruler's treasury as well as half of their personal gold.

Modcon
- Removed the modcon loading screen.
